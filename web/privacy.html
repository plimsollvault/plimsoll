Plimsoll has no accounts, no sign-up, no cookies, and no analytics. This page explains the little data that still passes through when you use the site.

In short

  • We do not ask for your name, email, or any other personal details.
  • Our web server keeps standard access logs for about two weeks.
  • Your wallet address is read in your browser and is never sent to us.
  • Blockchain transactions are public by design.

What our server logs

When you load plimsollvault.com, our web server records the request: your IP address, the page, the time, and your browser's user agent. We use these logs only to keep the site running and secure, and they are deleted on a rolling basis after about 14 days.

Your wallet address

When you connect a wallet, the console reads your address and balances directly from Robinhood Chain in your browser. We do not store it. Deposits, redemptions, and votes are public blockchain transactions that anyone can see, linked to your address, and they cannot be deleted.

Services the site loads

To work, your browser contacts these third parties, which receive your IP address and see their own part of the traffic:

  • Google Fonts, for the typefaces.
  • jsDelivr, which serves the ethers library.
  • Robinhood Chain RPC providers (Nodeflare, Robinhood, thirdweb, PublicNode), which answer blockchain reads and see the addresses queried.
  • Your wallet extension, under its own privacy policy.

Links to the block explorer, X, and GitHub open their sites under their own policies.

The agent

The agent sends market data to Anthropic's Claude API to plan each watch: prices, the vault's holdings, and its limits. It never sends anything about individual users.

Cookies and tracking

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.

Your choices

You can use the site without connecting a wallet. To avoid the third-party requests listed above, you can use the vault contract directly from the block explorer instead of this site.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. Questions go to @plimsollvault on X.

Plimsoll runs on Robinhood Chain (4663). Not an offer or investment advice. Stock tokens are issued by Robinhood, not by Plimsoll.
