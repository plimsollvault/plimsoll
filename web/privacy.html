Plimsoll has no accounts, no sign-up forms, no cookies and no analytics. This policy explains the limited data that still passes through when you use plimsollvault.com.

Summary

  • We never ask for your name, email, phone number or identity documents.
  • Our web server keeps standard access logs for about 14 days, only to run and protect the site.
  • Your wallet address is read in your browser and is not stored by us.
  • Deposits, redemptions and votes are public blockchain records that nobody can delete.
  • We do not sell or share data for advertising.

Who is responsible

The operators of Plimsoll ("we") are responsible for the data described here. You can reach us at @plimsollvault on X.

Server logs

When you load a page, our web server records the request: your IP address, the page requested, the time, the referring page, and your browser's user agent. We use these logs only to operate the site, diagnose errors and defend against abuse. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a secure website.

Wallet addresses

When you connect a wallet, the console reads your address, balances, shares and votes directly from Robinhood Chain inside your browser. This information is not sent to our server and we do not keep it. Disconnecting is as simple as closing the page or disconnecting the site in your wallet.

Public blockchain data

Transactions you make with the contracts are recorded on Robinhood Chain, where anyone can see them, linked to your address. The site's logbook and the agent read these public records. Because blockchains are permanent, neither we nor anyone else can change or delete them.

Services your browser contacts

To work, the site makes your browser contact these third parties. Each receives your IP address and sees its own part of the traffic, under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Fonts, which serves the typefaces;
  • jsDelivr, which serves the ethers library;
  • Robinhood Chain RPC providers (Robinhood, Nodeflare, thirdweb, PublicNode), which answer blockchain reads and see the addresses queried;
  • your wallet extension or app.

Links to the block explorer, X and GitHub take you to their sites, where their policies apply.

The agent

The agent sends market data to Anthropic's Claude API to plan each watch: stock prices, the vault's holdings and its limits. It does not send any information about individual holders.

Cookies and tracking

The site sets no cookies and uses no local storage for tracking. It runs no analytics, advertising, fingerprinting or social-media tracking scripts.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS with modern security headers. Server access is limited to the operators. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Retention

Server logs are deleted on a rolling basis after about 14 days, unless we need a specific entry longer to investigate abuse. We keep no other personal data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to its use. Because we hold only short-lived server logs and cannot link them to a person, we can usually only act on a request that identifies a specific time and IP address. Contact us on X to make a request. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

Plimsoll is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. Questions go to @plimsollvault on X.

Plimsoll runs on Robinhood Chain (4663). Not an offer or investment advice. Stock tokens are issued by Robinhood, not by Plimsoll.
