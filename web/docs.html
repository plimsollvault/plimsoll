Overview

Plimsoll is a shared vault on Robinhood Chain. You deposit USDG and receive vault shares. An AI agent sets the vault's mix of eight tokenized US stocks and USDG cash every 30 minutes. The vault contract checks every plan against fixed limits before anything trades, and the agent's key has no function that can move funds out.

Nothing here is held by a company or a person. Your share of the vault is a token in your own wallet, and you can redeem it at any time.

Depositing

  1. Open the console and connect a browser wallet such as MetaMask or Rabby. The console asks your wallet to switch to Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663).
  2. Enter an amount of USDG and choose Deposit. The first time, your wallet asks you to approve USDG for the vault, then to confirm the deposit.
  3. You receive PLIM shares in proportion to the vault's value. If the vault is worth 1.06 USDG per share, 1,000 USDG buys about 943.4 shares.

Deposits value every holding at the higher of its live pool price and its reference price. Moving a pool price down before depositing therefore never buys cheaper shares. Total deposits are capped at 10,000 USDG for now, and the cap can only be raised by a holder vote.

Redeeming

Redeeming burns your shares and pays you the same slice of every holding: USDG and each stock token. No prices are involved, so a redemption cannot be front-run or mispriced. Redemptions stay open even when deposits or trading are paused.

If a stock token refuses a transfer, for example because its issuer paused it, that part of your payout is recorded as owed to you. The console shows a Claim button for it, and you can collect it once the token moves again.

After redeeming you hold the stock tokens themselves. You can sell them on Uniswap or keep them, subject to the rules where you live.

Each watch

A watch is a 30-minute window. In each watch the agent can make one plan, in three steps:

  1. Seal. The agent asks Claude for target weights and a short reason, checks them against the mandate, and writes only a hash of the plan to the vault. Nobody, including the agent, can change the plan after that.
  2. Execute. At least four minutes later, the agent reveals the weights. The vault recomputes the hash, runs the mandate checks, and trades through the Uniswap pools in one transaction. If any check or any trade fails, the whole plan reverts and nothing moves.
  3. Reveal. In the next watch, the written reason is published on chain and must match the hash. The logbook shows it next to the outcome, so good and bad calls stay on the record.

The agent skips watches while the vault is empty, and holding the current mix is always an allowed plan.

The mandate

These limits are enforced by the vault contract, not by the agent. They are the launch values; a holder vote can change them.

Price protection

Every stock has a reference price stored in the vault. It is updated to the pool price after each executed plan, and anyone can nudge it toward the pool price by at most 5% per watch with syncRefPrice. Trades are refused while a pool price sits more than 5% from its reference, and each trade must return at least the amount implied by the reference price, less the pool fee and 1%.

Moving the reference price far therefore takes many watches of sustained pool prices, which makes a quick manipulation of a thin pool useless against the vault.

Storm brake

If the share price falls 20% below its peak over the last eight executed watches, the brake trips. From then on the only plan the vault accepts is 100% cash, and new deposits stop. Only a holder vote can reset it.

Holder votes

PLIM shares are also votes. To use them, turn on voting power once in the console; this delegates your votes to yourself and costs a small amount of gas.

  • Anyone with at least 100 shares of voting power can make a proposal.
  • Voting opens one hour after a proposal and lasts three days.
  • A proposal passes with more votes for than against and at least 10% of all shares voting.
  • A passed proposal waits 72 hours in the timelock before anyone can execute it. That gives holders who disagree time to redeem.

Proposals can change the limits, the deposit cap, the agent, the guardian, and pauses. They cannot add new assets or move holders' funds. Proposals are created on the governor contract; the repository explains how to encode one.

Guardian

The guardian can pause deposits or trading in an emergency. It cannot unpause, change limits, or touch funds. Unpausing takes a holder vote.

Risks

  • Stock prices can fall, and the vault can lose value even when every limit is respected.
  • The contracts have not been reviewed by an independent auditor. A bug could lose deposits.
  • Robinhood controls its stock tokens and can pause them, block addresses, or adjust balances.
  • Pools can run thin, which raises trading costs or blocks trades.
  • The agent can make poor calls within the limits. Its reasoning is published so holders can judge it.
  • Holding or trading tokenized stocks may be restricted where you live.

Read the terms before depositing.
