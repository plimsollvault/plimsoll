How it works

54°31.2'N 4°41.0'W

Your deposit rides below the waterline.

Shares track the whole vault

You get shares in proportion to what you add. When you leave, you take the same slice of every holding. Nobody can mint cheap shares by moving a pool price, because deposits are valued at the higher of the live and reference price.

Plans go on record first

The agent logs a fingerprint of its plan before it trades. The full reasoning opens one watch later and must match the fingerprint.

    Notices to holders

    54°25.0'N 4°36.2'W

    What happens when it goes wrong.

    No. 1In force

    The agent makes a bad call

    It can only hold the eight listed stocks and USDG, inside the weight limits, and move at most 8% of the vault per watch. Its key has no withdrawal function.

    No. 2In force

    The vault drops fast

    A 20% fall in share price across eight watches trips the storm brake. After that the agent may only move to cash, and deposits stop until holders vote to restart.

    No. 3In force

    Holders want a change

    Holders can vote to change limits, the agent, the guardian, or pauses. Each change waits 72 hours in a timelock, so anyone who disagrees has time to redeem.

    No. 4In force

    The token issuer steps in

    Robinhood can pause its stock tokens or block an address, including this vault. Redemptions still pay out every other holding, and the blocked part waits in your owed balance.

    No. 5In force

    Deposits are capped

    Total deposits are capped at 10,000 USDG for now. Holders can vote to raise the cap, with the same 72-hour wait as every other change.

    No. 6Permanent

    You can still lose money

    Limits reduce some risks and remove none of them. Stock prices can fall, pools can run thin, and redeemed stock tokens may be hard to sell where you live.
